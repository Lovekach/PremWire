## Reusable safeguards in the existing modules

- Frozen source-linked actor/origin/destination/status/payment slots; distinct
  metrics, units and periods. No names/roles/dates/fees generated by the model.
- Code-built editorial variants; Qwen selects 0/1 only, one batch call maximum.
  Invalid responses or timeout retain the correct deterministic draft, no retries.
- Source-local relation checks and whole-edition typed category/provenance gates.
  Explicit seller conflicts exclude affected actors, not the entire good edition.
- Transfer consolidation preserves independent reported currencies/statuses;
  official supplementary contract terms require the same explicit person/club.
- Cross-day signatures are actor/event scoped. Only proven printed facts enter
  legacy history; unprinted SOURCE details must not suppress a new development.
  New immutable editions still need their validated signature ledger at rollout.
- An explicit relative match date is anchored to its original SOURCE timestamp;
  first late-night coverage is not automatically an old-news duplicate.
- Final body and HTML/form transport round-trip validation before payload
  preparation; no network sender or production ledger used in shadow.
- Finite hidden isolated Ollama with owned-process cleanup; no live unload.

No mandatory entity registry or name whitelist was introduced. Translation
aliases are presentation metadata; unsupported relations remain explicit
omissions rather than guessed stories.
